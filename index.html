four

A live sampler for norns with four stereo tracks. Catch what your synth or drum machine plays, and the recording turns into a new instrument in your hands.

Scroll to put it together

This is a pocket-sized version of the norns shield, built around a Raspberry Pi Compute Module 4. It is still in design. four runs on any norns.

Switch it on.

Twelve pages, three keys, three encoders

E1 turns the page. K2, K3, E2 and E3 do what the bottom of the screen says, so the screen is the manual. Hold K1 for a second layer.

On every page

K1 + E1
Pick track 1 to 4. TAKE, VOICE, FILTER and EFFECT follow it.
K1 + K2
Stop everything, and start it again where it was.
K1 + K3
A tap turns every effect off. A long hold erases what the page holds.

Inside four

four runs on the SuperCollider engine of norns, with its own DSP written in C++ wherever the stock parts were too heavy or not good enough. All of it has to fit in one core of a Raspberry Pi 4.

The path of a sound

  1. InputAnything at line level, in stereo.
  2. TakeUp to 60 seconds of stereo, one for each of the four tracks.
  3. VoicePLAY, GRAIN, WARP, PV or CLOUD.
  4. FilterA diode ladder: low-pass, high-pass or band-pass.
  5. ColourDrive and crush.
  6. EffectsTwo in a row: chorus, flanger, wobble, resonator, ring or echo.
  7. SendsTo another track, the delay and the reverb.
  8. Delay and reverbA bucket brigade delay and a room, shared by every track.
  9. MixThe tracks against the tape loop, then a limiter.
  10. OutputStereo, at the level of the take when nothing is added.

The steps from the take to the sends run once for each track. The tape deck is softcut, norns' second audio engine, so the loop deck costs the main engine nothing.

The engine

Our own UGens
FourPV, FourWarp, FourRead, FourGrain, FourDelay, FourBBD, FourRoom and FourTanh, in one plugin. Without it, four builds stock voices and still plays.
The budget
One SuperCollider thread on a Raspberry Pi 4 starts to drop audio near 60%. Measured with reverb and delay on, four tracks of PLAY take 17%, four of GRAIN 19% and four of PV 29%.
Exact where it can be
At pitch 1 and speed 1, PLAY, WARP and PV return the take sample for sample, and a track leaves the engine at the level of its take.
Tested
The C++ cores have their own test program, and an offline check renders the real engine after every change and compares it with measured numbers.
